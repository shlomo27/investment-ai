DESCRIPTION
Investment AI researches US stocks for you and tells you, in plain language, what the analysis found and when it changes.

AI RESEARCH
Each recommendation comes from a team of AI analysts covering fundamentals, valuation, news, insider activity and risk. You get a bottom line, a target price, a stop loss and the reasoning behind them, plus a confidence level that is explained rather than just shown.

TECHNICAL SIGNALS
Candlestick charts with moving averages, Bollinger Bands, support and resistance, Fibonacci levels, RSI and MACD. Each stock gets a clear signal (Buy, Wait or Sell) and the price level that would change it.

FOLLOW AND GET ALERTED
Follow the stocks you care about. You get an alert when a signal changes, when a stock reaches an entry point, or when a price nears its stop loss or target, in the app and optionally on Telegram.

AN HONEST TRACK RECORD
Every past recommendation is tracked against the S&P 500, wins and losses alike.
