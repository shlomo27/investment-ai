Account & Data Deletion

Investment AI

From inside the app

  1. Sign in to your account.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Scroll to Delete account.
  4. Enter your password and type DELETE to confirm.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

If you cannot sign in

Email shlomo2708@gmail.com from the address registered on the account, with the subject "Account deletion". We respond within 30 days and confirm once done.

What is deleted

  • Account details — name, email, phone, hashed password
  • Investment preferences and risk profile
  • Portfolio and marked positions
  • Watchlist
  • Alert history
  • Device push token and Telegram link

What remains

The stock analyses themselves are not owned by your account — they are shared across all users and contain nothing that identifies you, so they stay in the system.

Non-identifying technical logs may persist briefly for security purposes.
